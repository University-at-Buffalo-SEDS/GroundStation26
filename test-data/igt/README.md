# IGT mock-flight datasets

Replay data for the integrated ground test (docs/integrated-ground-test.md):
each folder's `IGT/` directory goes to the root of the FC's SD card
(docs/integrated-ground-test.md section 11, "Using the committed mock flights").
Generated by `tools/flight_profile/generate.py --all` from
`tools/flight_profile/vehicle.json` (assumptions with sources) and
`tools/flight_profile/variants.json`; regenerating gives identical files.

t is seconds after T0, the FC's acceptance of Launch (ignition at 10 s, as on
the stand). Altitudes are above the pad; site 31.758, -102.046, 890.016 m MSL.
The vehicle is the team's OpenRocket design SWEET_V6
(`tools/flight_profile/vehicle/SWEET_V6.ork`: 4.39 m x 156 mm, 3 fins,
boattail, 40.84 kg dry with the motor hardware, as measured).
The motor is the 2026-11-16 static fire (DAQ thrust cell plus the fill-cell
unloading, 9.19 kN s, `tools/flight_profile/motors/testfire_20261116.json`). Reaching 10,000 ft
with the 40.84 kg dry mass needs about 14.4 kN s, so the 10k
sets scale the measured curve (shape kept) by the square root of the impulse
ratio on both the thrust and the time axis (a larger tank with a somewhat
larger injector), and the propellant at the measured delivered Isp. `measured_motor` flies the measured motor
unscaled.


Solved with the model (same seed):

- dry mass that reaches 3048 m with the measured motor: none: the measured motor peaks at about 3415 m near 15 kg dry (lighter airframes are drag-limited); 5 kg: 3063 m, 10 kg: 3336 m, 15 kg: 3415 m, 20 kg: 3278 m, 25 kg: 2830 m, 40.84 kg: 1518 m
- total impulse that reaches 3048 m with this airframe (measured curve shape): 14414 N s
- apogee with a full 8 kg N2O fill (impulse and propellant scaled by 8/5.6 = 13.1 kN s): 2667 m

Event times are the truth model's. The scripted deployments are what the FC is expected to command (drogue about 1.2 s after apogee, main 0.3 s after crossing 457.2 m), except where a variant says otherwise; the replay is open loop, so the FC's own pyro decisions do not change the data. Each folder's summary.json has the full numbers, seed and glitch list.

**early_apogee_noise**: baro raw noise 6 Pa (3x), static-port Cp -0.03 subsonic / -0.12 transonic (reads high during boost), 1200 Pa ejection pulses (about -110 m at the drogue), plus:
- `{"sensor": "baro", "kind": "outliers", "at": "liftoff", "dur_s": 60.0, "pa": 150.0, "prob": 0.02}`
- `{"sensor": "baro", "kind": "spike", "at": "apogee-8", "dur_s": 0.4, "pa": 350.0}`
- `{"sensor": "baro", "kind": "noise", "at": "apogee-5", "dur_s": 10.0, "scale": 3.0}`

**off_nominal**: the main physically opens at 300 m instead of 457 m (the FC still commands it at 457 m; the data keeps the drogue rate down to 300 m), with a harder opening, plus:
- `{"sensor": "imu", "kind": "spike", "at": "burnout+6", "dur_s": 0.01, "accel_mps2": [400.0, 0.0, -400.0], "gyro_dps": [0.0, 2500.0, 0.0]}`
- `{"sensor": "baro", "kind": "stuck", "at": "drogue+40", "dur_s": 2.0}`
- `{"sensor": "gps", "kind": "dropout", "at": "drogue+30", "dur_s": 15.0}`
- `{"sensor": "gps", "kind": "jump", "at": "main-20", "dur_s": 1.0, "east_m": 60.0}`
