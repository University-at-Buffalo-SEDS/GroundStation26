Load-cell temperature calibration

Leave the load cell unloaded. Allow the board and load cell to stabilize at each temperature, then capture. Use at least two temperatures 5 °C apart, preferably spanning the operating range. Repeat during cooling to check repeatability.

After fitting temperature compensation, repeat ordinary zero and mass calibration. ADC die temperature is a proxy for load-cell temperature; this corrects repeatable zero drift, not creep or sensitivity drift.
